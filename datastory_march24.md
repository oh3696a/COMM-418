# [War has sent thousands of planes flying in the other direction](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/03/12/business/iran-war-flight-diversions.html) #

This story from the New York Times illustrates how the Iran War and other conflicts in the Eastern Hemisphere have disrupted flight paths around the world. The Russia-Ukraine war and the new war in Iran are forcing planes to take odd, zig-zagging routes, which naturally means that the flights take longer and burn more fuel. Flight numbers themselves are also down, causing travel disruptions for many people in the Middle East.

My favorite part of the story is how simple it is; it only has one type of visualization (repeated a few times) that hammers home the same point: flight paths look a lot different when you can't fly over certain airspaces.

If I could change one thing about the story, I would ask for more data and visualizations about flight numbers and wait times. There definitely should be data about these things, and I think it would give a more complete picture about what flying in that area of the world looks like right now.
