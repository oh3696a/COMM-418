# [What if Barry Bonds played baseball without a bat?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JwMfT2cZGHg) #

This video is brought to you by the same genius who produced, "Ranking FBS Mascots by Weight." Once again, it's not hard-hitting journalism, but it's definitely entertaining.

I appreciate any story that takes a seemingly impossible hypothetical question and actually attempts to use math to answer it. In this video, Bois uses statistics to try to determine how effective Barry Bonds would have been at batting if he had never actually swung the bat. Obviously, the math isn't perfect, but it's a cool way to think about a ridiculous question.
