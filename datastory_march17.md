# [The Browns Live in Hell](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_gZndxEvFNk) #
I've had a rough few weeks, so I need to do a story about something that gets me cheered up, and making fun of Ohio is just what the doctor ordered. 
This story is about the hilarious ineptitude of the NFL's Cleveland Browns, and it takes a deep dive into all of their failings as a franchise. I'm impressed with how many metrics Bois used in painting this picture. He shows both how statistically bad the team is and how invisible they are to the nation.
If there's anything I would change about the video, I would further explore how other recent expansion teams have fared. He does a little bit of this with a few graphs, but I would love a deeper dive.
