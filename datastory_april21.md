# [Jeff Francoeur: My Favorite Worst Baseball Player](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VtKxqbwpm0) #

It's the last week of class, so I thought I would do another fun data story. This one is about Jeff Francoeur, a baseball player who has had one of the strangest careers of all time. After getting off to one of the greatest starts in MLB history, he just completely fell off the deep end and became one of the least valuable players of all time.

What I really appreciate about this story is that he goes off on his own whims and explores little tangents that just make the story even more interesting. A normal reporter may not include the fact that "Tim Foli once bit a guy," but Bois has an eye for the strange. 

If there's anything I would have done differently in his shoes, it would be reaching out to Francoeur himself. Traditionally, Bois hasn't interviewed the athletes he's talked about, and it would have been difficult to secure an interview with an MLB athlete, but Francoeur is such a nice guy that he may have actually had a shot. Talking to Francoeur probably would have given Bois some even better insight and made the narrative part of the story better.
