# MY FIRST COMM-418 CLASS #
Today's Data Journalism class featured a basic introduction to the fundamentals of [the course](https://american.instructure.com/courses/77635) and a quick exploration of Google Sheets and Github/Markdown. Using responses from a class survey, we created a master sheet and drew conclusions about the data.

One of the pieces I found most interesting -- perhaps comforting -- was that most of us had little experience with the technical aspects of the course (query, programming/scripting languages, etc). There are a few people in the class with more experience, and they will hopefully help the less fortunate, but I'm imagining the course will go slow enough to accommodate the majority of us.

Another takeaway I had from the data was that I'm somewhat behind on my experience with FOIA. As a sports journalist, I don't see myself having to use it often, but at my core, I don't like knowing less than other people, so perhaps I should become more experienced with it.
