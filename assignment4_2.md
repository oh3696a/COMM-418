# Is Kalshi secretly advancing the Republican agenda? #
Kalshi is a platform for prediction markets, a form of trading (or betting, depending on how one looks at it) that allows users to purchase "contracts" on yes/no questions. The platform has exploded in popularity in the last year, and it has drawn controversy along with that popularity due to its shaky legal status -- many states view it as an unregulated sportsbook and want to ban it.

One of the touted benefits of Kalshi and other prediction markets is that they enhance "informed decision-making and public understanding," as users who are willing to bet money on current events will supposedly have a thorough understnading of those current events. This has traditionally been a responsibility of the news media, and Kalshi knows that, which is why it's partnering with news agencies like CNN and CNBC to integrate its markets into their coverage. 

If Kalshi is going to operate in some capacity as a news service, it should be judged with similar scrutiny, and from a quick glance at their Instagram account, it appears that the platform has a Republican bias. None of its posts are overtly opinionated, but they more often than not focus on negative news regarding prominent Democrats. It's unlikely that this is pure coincidence; Donald Trump, Jr. is a paid advisor to Kalshi.
