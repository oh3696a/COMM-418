# The Tree Canopy Divide in DC: A Study in Environmental Justice #

_A quick note: This idea is subject to change_

In an earlier assignment, I found that there is a distinct gap in tree coverage between wards in Washington, D.C. I want to expand upon this finding and go deeper to explore what these findings mean for environmental justice. I would narrow my scope of analysis from wards to ANCs or census tracts and examine how zoning (residential vs. industrial vs. special use) might confound my findings.

With my data, I want to find out what areas are underserved in terms of tree canopy coverage, what those areas have in common (demographics, history, etc), and why they are underserved. Interviews will obviously be crucial for this story; they'll give context to my findings, help explain the forces behind them, and explain why they matter.

This will definitely be a multimedia story. I plan on using plenty of maps and graphs to make my findings easier to understand, and I also want to take photos so that people can see what an underserved area (in terms of tree cover) looks like. At the least, I want the story to open some eyes; in an ideal world, it could even lead to new local initiatives.
